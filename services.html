Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start the home buying process?

Start by scheduling a free consultation with me. We'll discuss your budget, preferred neighborhoods, and must-have features. I'll help you get pre-qualified for financing and then start showing you properties that match your criteria.

What is the typical timeline for selling a home?

In Addis Ababa's current market, well-priced homes typically sell within 2-6 weeks. Factors like location, condition, and pricing strategy affect this timeline. I use data-driven pricing and strategic marketing to help you sell quickly at the best price.

Do you work with international buyers/investors?

Absolutely! I work with clients from around the world looking to invest in Addis Ababa real estate. I can provide virtual tours, handle documentation, and guide you through the foreign investment process.

What neighborhoods offer the best investment returns?

Currently, areas like Sarbet, Bole Extension, and certain parts of Kazanchis are showing strong appreciation. However, the best investment depends on your budget, timeline, and goals. I can provide specific ROI data for any area you're considering.
