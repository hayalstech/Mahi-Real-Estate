Before You Contact

Quick answers to common questions

Do I need to pre-qualify for a mortgage before contacting you?

Not necessarily! I can connect you with trusted lenders to get pre-qualified. However, having a pre-qualification letter makes your offer stronger when we find the right property.

How much does it cost to work with you?

For buyers - my services are completely free (commission is paid by the seller). For sellers, I charge a competitive commission rate that includes full marketing, staging advice, and transaction management.

Can you help me if I'm outside of Addis Ababa?

Absolutely! I work with clients from all over Ethiopia and abroad. I can provide virtual tours, video calls, and handle all paperwork remotely until you're ready to visit.
